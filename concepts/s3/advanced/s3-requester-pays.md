## Amazon S3 Requester Pays
Make object-requesters pay for networking cost, instead of object owner.

### Features
#### general
- in general, bucket owners pay for all amazon s3 storage and data transfer costs associated with their bucket
- with requester pays bucket, the requester instead of the bucker owner pays the cost of the request and the data downloaded from the bucket
- helpful when you want to share large datasets with other accounts
- the requester must be authenticated in AWS (can not be anonymous)
