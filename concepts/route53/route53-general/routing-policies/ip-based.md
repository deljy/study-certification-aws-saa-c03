## IP-based Routing Policy
a type of routing policies that Route53's record can have

### Features
#### general
- Routing is based on client's IP addresses
- you provide a list of CIDRs for your clients, and corresponding endpoints/locations (user IP to endpoint mappings)
- use cases:
    - optimise performance
    - reduce network costs
- examples:
    - route end users from a particular ISP to a specific endpoint
