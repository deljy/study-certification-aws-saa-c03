## CloudFront Pricing
short explanation comes here

### Features
#### general
- since CloudFront edge locations are all around the world, the cost of data out peer edge location varies. 

#### Price Classes
- description:
    - you can reduce the number of edge locations in order to reduce the cost
- classes:
    - price class all:
        - all regions.
    - price class 200:
        - most regions excluding the most expensive regions.
        - expluding australia and south america
    - price class 100:
        - only the least expensive regions
        - including only europe and north america
